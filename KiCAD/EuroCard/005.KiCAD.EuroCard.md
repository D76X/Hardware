# What are the advantages to use EuroCard for a PCB?

Using the Eurocard format for a printed circuit board (PCB) offers a variety of advantages, 
primarily centered around mechanical standardization, interchangeability, and integration 
into industrial rack systems. 

[1] (https://uk.rs-online.com/web/content/product-knowledge/computing-networking/computer-hardware-components/eurocards-pcb-guide), 

[2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board))

Originally introduced to standardize metric board sizes in Europe, it has become a global standard 
(governed by `IEC-60297-3` and `IEEE 1101.10`) for high-reliability systems. 

[1] (https://www.multi-circuit-boards.eu/en/support/glossary/eurocard.html), 
[2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board))

## Core Advantages

- Off-the-Shelf Compatibility: 

Because dimensions are completely standardized (e.g., standard 3U is 100mm x 160mm), 
you do not need to design custom enclosures. Compatible subracks, chassis, card guides, 
and 19-inch racks are widely available from multiple vendors. 

[1] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board)), 
[2] (https://www.multi-circuit-boards.eu/en/support/glossary/eurocard.html), 
[3] (https://peakservo.com/products/what-is-a-eurocard/), 
[4] (https://uk.rs-online.com/web/content/product-knowledge/computing-networking/computer-hardware-components/eurocards-pcb-guide)

- Modular System Scaling: 

Eurocards are designed to slide into subrack slots like books on a shelf. 
This modularity allows engineers to build, expand, or upgrade a system simply by plugging in 
new cards or swapping out old ones. 

[1] (https://sdiy.info/wiki/Eurocard), 
[2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board)), 
[3] (https://uk.rs-online.com/web/content/product-knowledge/computing-networking/computer-hardware-components/eurocards-pcb-guide)

- High-Reliability Connections: 

Eurocards traditionally mate with standardized two-piece architecture connectors, 
such as the `DIN 41612` or `high-speed 5-row DIN` connectors. 

These subrack backplane systems provide exceptional signal integrity, environmental resilience, 
and secure mechanical mating. 

[1] (https://www.te.com/en/products/connectors/pcb-connectors/backplane-connectors/eurocard-connectors.html), 
[2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board))

- Reduced Manufacturing Costs: 
Because PCB fabrication panel sizes and assembly machinery are widely optimized for Eurocard dimensions, manufacturing these standard-sized boards is highly economical and efficient. [1] (https://www.multi-circuit-boards.eu/en/support/glossary/eurocard.html)
• Excellent Thermal & Shock Management: Slotted card guides support the PCB along its entire top and bottom edges. This distribution of structural support provides excellent resistance to vibration and mechanical shock, while subrack configurations naturally optimize vertical airflow for cooling. Conduction-cooled variants are even standardized for harsh military/aerospace environments. [1] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lansing-instrument_pcbdesign-medicaldevices-eurocard-activity-7516181138093158402-mEHo), [2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board)), [3] (https://sdiy.info/wiki/Eurocard)

Common Industry Implementations

The format is so versatile that it serves as the underlying physical framework for many widespread industrial bus architectures and specialized electronic fields: [1] (https://www.te.com/en/products/connectors/pcb-connectors/backplane-connectors/eurocard-connectors.html), [2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocard_(printed_circuit_board))

Industry/Application	Associated Standards & Architectures
Industrial Computing & Datacom	VMEbus, VME64, CompactPCI, CompactPCI Express, Multibus-II
Instrumentation & Test Gear	PXI, PXI Express, VXI
Audio Electronics	The popular "Eurorack" format for modular music synthesizers
Aerospace & Defense	Conduction-cooled electronic modules (IEEE 1101.2)

# INTERNATIONAL STANDARD IEC-60297-3

IEC 60297-3 is a series of international standards that defines the mechanical 
dimensions, design aspects, and modular architectures for 482.6 mm (19-inch) 
electronic equipment structures. 

[1] (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/15882/dee9b74d54a1458dbd4896136e80d81f/IEC-60297-3-100-2008.pdf)

## Scope and Core Standard

- Base Standard: 
IEC 60297-3-100 specifies the foundational dimensions for 19-inch front panels, subracks, chassis, 
racks, and cabinets. 

[1] (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/15882/dee9b74d54a1458dbd4896136e80d81f/IEC-60297-3-100-2008.pdf)

- Height Increment: 
1U equals 44.45 mm (1.75 inches), with panel heights scaling from 1U upward. 

[1] (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/15882/dee9b74d54a1458dbd4896136e80d81f/IEC-60297-3-100-2008.pdf)

## Key Parts of the IEC 60297-3 Series

The series consists of various sub-standards covering modular components, specific chassis designs, 
and specialized applications:

- Subracks & Hardware (IEC 60297-3-101 to -104): Covers subracks, plug-in units, injector/extractor handles, keying/alignment pins, and connector-dependent interface dimensions. [1] (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/72827)

- Chassis & Specialized Enclosures (IEC 60297-3-105 to -107): Includes 1U chassis dimensions, metric adaptation dimensions, and small form-factor subracks. [1] (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/72827)

- Harsh Environments & Computing (IEC 60297-3-108 to -110): 
Addresses ruggedized R-type subracks, embedded computing chassis for industrial, medical, and 
transport use, as well as residential smart-home racks. 

[1] (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/21481/aff53f72d5fb404cb245cd97be41fa4f/IEC-60297-3-109-2015.pdf), 
[2] (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/72827), 
[3] (https://scc-ccn.ca/standardsdb/standards/2023771)

Official publications and previews can be accessed via the IEC Webstore. 

[1] (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/72827), 
[2] (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/1283)

# INTERNATIONAL STANDARD IEEE 1101.10 (IEEE Std 1101.10-1996)

IEEE 1101.10 (officially IEEE Std 1101.10-1996) is an IEEE standard that defines additional 
mechanical specifications for 19-inch equipment practices, extending the core Eurocard and 
microcomputer standards in IEEE 1101.1 (often used alongside VME64x systems). 

[1] (https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1101.10/1665/), 
[2] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

## Key Features

- EMC Shielding: 
Includes front panel gaskets and conductive side/rear elements to protect against electromagnetic interference. 

[1] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

- Injector / Extractor Handles: 
Mechanical handles that provide leverage for high-density, high-pin-count connectors requiring insertion/extraction forces up to 175 lbs. 

[1] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

- Slot Keying: 
Mechanical keying systems offering up to 4,096 unique programming variations to prevent mis-insertion 
of plug-in units. 

[1] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

- Alignment Pins & ESD Protection: 
Features alignment pins for parallel mating and electrostatic discharge (ESD) contacts/clips integrated into card guides. 

[1] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

- Safety Covers: 
Optional or protective side covers for enhanced mechanical and electrical safety. 

[1] (https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21774173/a-guide-to-dot-ten-ieee-110110)

## Status

- Approved: July 1996 (Published November 1996, ANSI approved May 1997)
- Current Status: Inactive-Reserved Standard as of November 7, 2019 

[1] (https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1101.10/1665/)

You can review the official status and details via the IEEE Standards Association. 
[1] (https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1101.10/1665/)
